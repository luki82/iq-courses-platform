How it works

  1. Pay once. Pay {{ paid_tests.0.price_display }} by card through Stripe. You don't need to create an account. One payment covers one attempt at the test.
  2. Save your private link. After paying, you get a private link to your test. Bookmark it: you can use it to start the test later or come back to your result on any device.
  3. Take the test. Answer 30 multiple-choice questions in {{ paid_tests.0.time_limit_minutes|default:30 }} minutes. The questions start easy and get harder, and cover:
    • pattern and matrix puzzles: find the shape, arrow or number that completes a grid
    • number problems: sequences, percentages and short word problems
    • word meaning: vocabulary, analogies and finding the odd one out
    • spatial thinking: folding paper, nets of cubes, rotations and mirror images
    • working memory: holding information in your head and re-ordering it
  4. Watch the clock. The timer starts when you open the test and keeps running even if you leave the page. When it reaches zero, your answers are submitted automatically, so answer every question, even if you have to guess.
  5. See your result. You'll see your score, how long you took and an estimated IQ-style score. You'll also get the correct answer and an explanation for every question.

Before you start

Find a quiet spot where you won't be interrupted for half an hour. Have a pen and paper ready. You won't need a calculator. A laptop or tablet makes the grid questions easier to read, but a phone works too.
