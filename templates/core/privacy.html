Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information iQ ("we", "us") collects when you use lorrank.com, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details. If you sign up, we store your username, email address and a securely scrambled (hashed) version of your password. We never see or store your actual password.
  • Learning activity. The courses you add, lessons you mark complete, and your IQ test answers, scores and times, so we can show you your progress and results.
  • Cookies needed to run the site. A session cookie keeps you logged in and remembers your test timer and results in your browser, and a security cookie protects forms against misuse.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.

Donations

Donations are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never stored on our servers. Stripe's handling of your information is covered by the Stripe Privacy Policy.

Advertising and analytics

We may show advertising to help keep iQ free, and may use analytics tools to understand how the site is used.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at www.aboutads.info.
  • You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

How we use your information

  • To provide the courses, tests and your results and progress.
  • To keep the site secure and fix problems.
  • To understand which content is useful so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • You can use the IQ test and read lessons without an account.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features (like staying logged in or your test timer) may then not work.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your account and its data by contacting us.

Children

iQ is intended for general audiences. If you believe a child has given us personal information without a parent's consent, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
