About iQ

iQ is a free learning site at lorrank.com. It has English courses for adults, IELTS preparation lessons and a timed IQ test, all free to use.

What you'll find here

  • English courses for beginners, the workplace and school students, with every word read aloud and drag-and-drop practice.
  • IELTS preparation across Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking, from foundation level to Band 9 model answers.
  • The iQ Challenge, a 30-question, 30-minute reasoning test with a full answer breakdown.

The name

Our logo comes from the lorrank, a plant (its name is from the Bari language) that sends thin runners between obstacles, then thickens into a canopy. Learning works the same way: small steady steps that grow into something strong.

Why it's free

We want anyone, anywhere, to be able to learn English and prepare for IELTS without paying. The site is kept online by donations and, in future, by advertising.
